# Storage and automation

## Read your stock

Open Matter Storage or a linked Matter Terminal. Search by substance name, then use the right-hand arrows to browse. Each entry has an amount and a phase. Liquid water, steam and solid water are separate stocks, even though they share a chemical identity.

Each Matter Storage holds up to **1,000,000 total units**. A processing machine uses exactly one connected storage. Adding a second storage to the same network makes the connection ambiguous; use separate networks instead.

## Enable external automation

Automation starts **off**. The owner enables it separately in each machine's menu.

- Items: pipes and hoppers may insert into the input and extract from the output.
- Upgrades: external automation cannot insert or remove them.
- Fluids: enable Automation for external water or lava pipes. Manual bucket interaction remains available to the owner.
- FE: energy can be supplied independently of the Automation setting.

The recipe and Run/Pause controls are set by the owner. Leave a machine running for repeated processing, or pause it before changing your setup.

## Ownership is deliberate

The player who places a machine owns it. Other players cannot open or mine it. A matter network joins machines of the **same owner** only.

Enabling Automation explicitly lets external pipes move resources; pipes have no player identity. Enable it only where you trust the connected automation. The switch does not give other players access to your menus.

## Move stored resources safely

Mining your machine keeps its items, fluids, matter and FE inside the dropped block. Place that block again to restore them. Unfinished work restarts with its inputs intact. Keep the dropped block safe: it is still a normal world item and can be lost like other valuables.
