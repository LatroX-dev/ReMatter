# A short link between your own loaded networks

<RecipeFor id="rematter:entangled_link" />

Place two Links and connect their local sides to your Matter Cable networks. Hold a Link item and **shift-right-click the first Link, then the second**. The on-screen message confirms selection and pairing.

Both endpoints must have the same owner, be in the **same dimension**, remain in already loaded chunks and stay within **32 blocks**. They must refer to each other. The pair never loads chunks or connects somebody else's stock.

It counts as **one Basic cable connection** with 128 u/t matter work and 2,048 FE/t native routing. Controller and source budgets still apply. It creates no new allowance per parallel lane, side or second route.

Removing, unloading or re-pairing an endpoint stops the old connection. Complete both loaded endpoints before expecting a route. This is a short workshop link, not dimensional storage.
