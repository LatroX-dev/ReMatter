# Two ways to research without a sample

Both modes live in the Analyzer's **Research** tab and spend its explicitly inserted Primal Cell. A nearby Drive is never an automatic payment account. Neither mode produces substances or a finished item.

## Derive a chosen material

The material must permit derivation, remain unknown, and have every required substance already known. The Primal price is four times the matter in its smallest permitted synthesizable form, clamped to **4,000–16,000 u**. Base FE is 256,000 and base duration 90 seconds. Normal upgrades may change FE and time, but never the Primal price. Known materials do not cost another derivation.

Derivation does not expand the synthesis allowlist. A decomposition-only family stays decomposition-only.

## Discovery is an unknown find

<RecipeFor id="rematter:discovery_lens" />

Install a Discovery Lens. Base requirements are **32 earned material families**, both a completed Industrial Reactor and Industrial Preparator, and the machine owner's required dimension visits. Administrative grants and starting knowledge do not count as earned play. Packs can deliberately change the threshold and curated target list.

Choose a **tier**. Read its actual price, minimum time and eligible remaining count. The specific family is a surprise until paid completion. Each eligible, still-locked family in that tier initially has equal chance. A high-tier project cannot return lower-tier filler.

| Tier | Primal | Base FE | Fixed minimum time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 16,000 u | 2.4 million FE | 5 minutes |
| II | 32,000 u | 4.8 million FE | 10 minutes |
| III | 64,000 u | 9.6 million FE | 20 minutes |

Saved payment steps use 4,000 u and 600,000 base FE. The window shows FE after upgrades. Speed cannot shorten Discovery's minimum duration. Neither sample nor known target substances are required. Machines, equipment, manufactured parts and unapproved special rewards remain excluded.

## Pause without rerolling

Pause, reopening, changing machines and reloading do not generate a new hidden candidate order. The saved project belongs to its original research identity and tier. Another authorized Analyzer can resume it. Owner/team or incompatible data changes pause safely.

A candidate learned by another route is skipped in favour of the next eligible unknown family of that tier. An empty pool before starting costs nothing. If all remaining candidates become known during work, no further Primal is charged and paid Primal is refunded **once**. A full or missing cell leaves a saved refund claim. Spent FE and time are not refunded. Missing definitions pause for correction.
