## How the sounds were made

The sound clips in `artwork/sound-sets/` were produced for ReMatter. Tonal, motion and
digital layers are synthesised by the project's own tooling. Foley layers are cut from
audio generated locally with the Apache-2.0 licensed text-to-audio model
MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0; the prompts are recorded in each set's `set.json`. The clips contain
no third-party recordings and no Minecraft sounds.
